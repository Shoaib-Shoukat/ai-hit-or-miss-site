Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

AI Hit or Miss Studio ("the Tool") is a private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel AI Hit or Miss (@AIHitOrMiss) to manage that channel. This policy explains what data the Tool accesses and how it is handled.

YouTube API Services

The Tool uses YouTube API Services. By using the Tool you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the Tool accesses

  • YouTube Data API: uploading videos, setting thumbnails and metadata (title, description, tags) on the owner's own channel; reading the owner's channel information; searching public YouTube videos (titles, view counts, public channel subscriber counts) to identify trending topics.
  • YouTube Analytics API: reading aggregate performance metrics (views, watch time, retention, subscribers gained) for the owner's own channel only.

How data is used, stored and shared

  • Data is used only to produce, publish and improve videos for the owner's channel.
  • The OAuth access token and aggregated metrics are stored locally on the owner's computer. They are not sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party.
  • Public video metadata collected for topic research is stored as aggregated notes and refreshed or deleted within 30 days.
  • The Tool does not collect data from viewers, other users or other channels' private data, and it does not use cookies.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After revocation, locally stored tokens stop working and are deleted.
